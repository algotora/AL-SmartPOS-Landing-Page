AL SmartPOS is a point-of-sale (POS) app for small shops, tuckshops, and general dealers — it replaces your till, stock book and ledger with one app on the phone or laptop you already own. How it works

From install to first sale in four steps

No setup call, no training session. Most shop owners are ringing up their own sales within the hour.

STEP 01

Install once

Install AL SmartPOS on the PC, laptop or Android device — ideally a tablet — that will stay at the till. It's a one-time install; after that it opens straight from the home screen or desktop, no data plan required.

STEP 02

Ring up the sale

Add items from your stock list and charge the customer in USD, ZiG or Rand — the total converts automatically, at the exchange rate you set.

STEP 03

Hand over the receipt

Print to a thermal printer or send a PDF straight to the customer's WhatsApp — either way, it's on record.

STEP 04

Watch the shop run itself

A dashboard flags what's expiring, what's not moving, and which cashier sold what.

Before you install

Straight answers, no fine print

Sideloading an app (installing outside the Play Store) is normal for small local software, but it's fair to ask questions first. Here's exactly what's going on.

Why isn't this on the Play Store?

Play Store listings take fees and review time that small local tools like this skip by distributing directly. Sideloading is the same install method used by banking-adjacent apps and enterprise tools across Africa — Android just asks you to confirm it once.

Where does my sales data go?

Nowhere but your device. AL SmartPOS is offline-first — your stock, sales and customer records are stored locally on your phone or laptop, not on our servers. That's also why it keeps working without a data bundle.

What if I lose my phone?

Backup & restore is included free — download a backup file any time from Settings, or set an auto-backup interval, and restore it onto a new device to bring back your products, sales history and settings.

Get started

On your phone in under two minutes

  1. Tap Download on this page to save the app file.
  2. Open the file. Android will show a standard "install from unknown sources" prompt — that's Android being cautious by default, not a warning about this app. Allow it; it's a one-time approval.
  3. Log in with the default credentials — Admin username admin, password/PIN 1234; or Cashier PIN 1111. Change these under Settings → Manage Users once you're in.
  4. Open AL SmartPOS and start ringing up your first sale, free.
