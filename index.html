Before you install

Straight answers, no fine print

Why isn't this on the Play Store?

Play Store listings take fees and review time that small local tools skip by distributing directly. Android will ask you to confirm the install once — that's normal, not a warning about this app.

Where does my sales data go?

Nowhere but your device. AL SmartPOS is offline-first — stock, sales and customer records stay on your phone or laptop, not on a server.

What if I lose my phone?

Backup & restore is included free. Download a backup any time from Settings, or set an auto-backup, and restore it onto a new device.

Who do I talk to if something goes wrong?

A real person on WhatsApp — 0772 420 512. Built and supported by Al Leath Gotora in Zimbabwe.
