Questions

Everything you need to know.

Does AL SmartPOS work without internet?

Yes. The core POS is designed to work offline, so you can continue recording sales and managing the shop when there is no data connection.

Is the Free version really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit. It provides the core POS for one shop, including sales, products, expenses, reports, multi-currency support and offline operation.

What does the $5 Premium upgrade do?

Premium adds features for businesses that need more control or are growing, including unlimited shops and cashiers, quotations, supplier and purchase tracking, stock auditing, advanced reports, credit tracking and Telegram alerts.

Where is my business data stored?

AL SmartPOS is offline-first. Core business records are stored locally on the device rather than requiring a cloud account to operate. Use the built-in backup and restore tools to protect your data.

Can I use it on an Android tablet?

Yes. Android phones and tablets are supported. A tablet can be especially useful at a shop counter because the larger screen makes checkout easier.

What are the default login credentials?

Admin logs in with username admin and PIN 1234. Cashier accounts use PIN 1111. Both can be changed at any time from Settings once you're logged in.

How do I get help or unlock Premium?

Contact AL SmartPOS on WhatsApp at 0772 420 512. Premium can be arranged directly through WhatsApp.
